# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Release Managers / Delivery Leads

### Role Summary
Release Managers or Delivery Leads coordinate release planning and readiness, from deployment scheduling through rollback and go/no-go decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release plans, deployment windows, and production readiness
- Maintain rollback criteria and mitigation plans
- Track release scope, dependencies, and go/no-go decisions
- Confirm that required evidence and approvals are complete before release

### Goals
- Deliver releases predictably with minimal operational risk
- Make release readiness and rollback decisions transparent
- Improve coordination across engineering, quality, product, and operations

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and go/no-go check-ins
- Deployment plans and rollback procedures
- Release status updates and stakeholder announcements

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers on schedules, risks, and cross-team dependencies.
- Coordinates with Developers on release content and technical readiness.
- Reviews acceptance evidence with QA/Quality Leads.
- Aligns scope and stakeholder messaging with Product Managers.
- Partners with Operations/Support Leads on deployability, runbooks, and recovery.

---

## UX/Design Leads

### Role Summary
UX/Design Leads shape usable, accessible experiences and translate customer and product needs into clear interaction and visual requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Define and validate user flows, interaction patterns, and design specifications
- Set accessibility expectations and design acceptance criteria
- Facilitate design feedback and resolve usability tradeoffs
- Validate that implemented experiences match agreed designs

### Goals
- Improve usability and consistency across product experiences
- Ensure accessibility is considered throughout delivery
- Reduce rework caused by unclear or unvalidated design decisions

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and usability sessions
- Annotated flows, prototypes, and design acceptance criteria
- Accessibility and implementation handoffs

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partners with Product Managers on problem framing, customer needs, and desired outcomes.
- Works with Developers to confirm feasibility and clarify implementation details.
- Collaborates with QA/Quality Leads on usability and accessibility validation.
- Provides Project Managers with design dependencies and validation milestones.

---

## QA/Quality Leads

### Role Summary
QA/Quality Leads define and maintain the testing approach that provides confidence in feature quality and release readiness.

### Responsibilities
- Own test strategy and quality gates
- Coordinate unit, integration, end-to-end, and manual acceptance testing
- Lead defect triage and track quality risks
- Verify that implemented work meets acceptance criteria

### Goals
- Prevent defects from reaching production
- Make quality status visible and evidence-based
- Improve testability and feedback speed across the delivery lifecycle

### Typical Communication
- Test plans and quality reports
- Defect triage and acceptance reviews
- Risk updates in planning and delivery syncs

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers on testability, test coverage, and defect resolution.
- Partners with Product Managers to clarify acceptance criteria and important edge cases.
- Keeps Project Managers informed of quality risks, blockers, and release readiness.
- Confirms release evidence with Release Managers and coordinates usability checks with UX/Design Leads.

---

## Security/Compliance Leads

### Role Summary
Security/Compliance Leads ensure that security, privacy, and regulatory requirements are identified and addressed throughout project delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Define applicable security and compliance requirements
- Review threats, risks, controls, and mitigation plans
- Coordinate security and compliance reviews
- Advise teams on incident response and remediation readiness

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance exposure
- Make risk decisions and required controls explicit
- Enable timely remediation and safe releases

### Typical Communication
- Security and threat-model reviews
- Compliance and risk assessments
- Incident readiness and escalation guidance

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Consulted by Developers during design and implementation.
- Advises Product Managers on risk tradeoffs and customer or regulatory constraints.
- Provides Project Managers with release gates, risk owners, and escalation needs.
- Coordinates with Release Managers and Operations/Support Leads on secure deployment and incident response.

---

## Operations/Support Leads

### Role Summary
Operations/Support Leads prepare for production operation, support customer needs after release, and feed operational evidence back into the project backlog.

### Responsibilities
- Prepare operational runbooks and support coverage
- Monitor post-release health and recurring customer issues
- Coordinate incident response, escalation, and follow-up actions
- Surface operational and support feedback for future prioritization

### Goals
- Improve production reliability and support readiness
- Shorten incident detection and recovery time
- Ensure customer feedback influences subsequent delivery

### Typical Communication
- Operational readiness reviews and support handoffs
- Incident summaries and post-release health updates
- Backlog feedback and known-issue reports

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Coordinates with Developers and Release Managers on deployability, recovery, and root-cause follow-up.
- Provides Product Managers with recurring customer pain points and operational impact.
- Gives Project Managers incident status, dependencies, and action-item updates.
- Works with QA/Quality Leads on production validation and support escalation paths.

---

## User Researchers / Analytics Partners

### Role Summary
User Researchers and Analytics Partners provide evidence about customer needs and whether delivered work achieves intended outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and conduct customer research
- Analyze product usage, experiment, and outcome metrics
- Validate measurement plans and success criteria
- Communicate findings and evidence to delivery teams

### Goals
- Ground project decisions in customer and usage evidence
- Demonstrate whether outcomes improve after delivery
- Reduce uncertainty in prioritization and validation

### Typical Communication
- Research findings and customer insights
- Measurement plans and experiment results
- Outcome reviews and success-metric updates

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Supplies customer and market evidence to Product Managers.
- Defines measurement plans with Developers and QA/Quality Leads.
- Gives Project Managers insight into validation risks and milestone evidence.
- Collaborates with UX/Design Leads to validate usability and translate findings into design improvements.
