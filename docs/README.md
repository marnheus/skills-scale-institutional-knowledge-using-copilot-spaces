# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

These documents describe how OctoAcme plans and delivers cross-functional product features, services, and integrations. The approach is customer-first and iterative, with clear ownership, measurable outcomes, and continuous learning.

## Project management processes

OctoAcme begins by validating the business need, success measures, stakeholders, risks, and whether work should proceed. Approved initiatives move into planning, where teams prioritize a backlog, define acceptance criteria and the Definition of Done, estimate work, and agree on milestones, dependencies, and a release plan.

During execution, teams deliver in small increments and track work on a project board from backlog through review, QA, and completion. Pull requests should be small where possible, linked to issues and acceptance criteria, and reviewed with automated tests and linting passing. Quality practices include unit and applicable integration tests, end-to-end smoke tests for critical flows, security scanning, and manual QA when needed. Releases require completed acceptance criteria, passing CI and security checks, release notes, a rollback or mitigation plan, and post-deployment verification.

The Project Manager coordinates delivery, risks, and communication; the Product Manager owns outcomes, prioritization, and success measures; developers build and test; QA validates quality and acceptance; and stakeholders provide input and approvals. Teams use standups, weekly delivery or PM syncs, regular stakeholder updates, and sprint or milestone demos to stay aligned. Risks are monitored and escalated as needed, while retrospectives after sprints, releases, or incidents turn lessons into owned improvement actions.
